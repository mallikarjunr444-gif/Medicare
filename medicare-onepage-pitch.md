# MediCare — Talk to a Doctor. In Your Language.

**Voice-first AI healthcare companion for the next half-billion Indians · Medicus Labs™**

## The Problem

India has ~1 doctor per 1,000 people, yet digital health tools are **English-only, text-only and text-heavy**. Millions in Tier-2/3 India can't reach a doctor — and mental health carries too much stigma to ask.

## The Solution

**MediCare — a free, no-signup web app: talk by voice (English, Hindi, Kannada) to a photorealistic AI doctor; clear preliminary guidance in ~5 minutes.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Dr. Smit — AI General Physician** | Symptom triage, differentials, home care, red-flag detection |
| **Dr. Ananya — AI Mental Wellness Companion** | Empathetic support, guided breathing, 24/7 crisis escalation |
| **Lab reports + session summaries** | Plain-language read, save & share with a real doctor — AI bridges, never replaces |

## Why We Win

- **Vernacular voice-first**: Whisper large-v3-turbo + AI4Bharat IndicConformer STT, native Hindi/Kannada voices (IndicF5, MOS 4.6) — no English fallback.
- **Clinically grounded**: dual RAG — 11,274 NIH/MedlinePlus guides (MedQuAD) kept separate from counselling corpora (ESConv, CACTUS, MIT Psychology); answers traceable to source.
- **Safety engineered, not promised**: 4-level deterministic triage every turn; red alerts auto-connect to 112 / KIRAN / Tele-MANAS; no prescriptions, no autonomous diagnosis — 99.4% pass on 1,350 red-team probes.
- **Live & lean**: Qwen 2.5 backbone, self-hosted models, live on free-tier today — full engineering public on GitHub.
